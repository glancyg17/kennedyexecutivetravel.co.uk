The experience

Here's what happens.

Every booking is a full VIP experience at your front door. The Elves arrive first, lead the children to the Rudolph-themed vehicle, and the automatic door opens into Santa's Grotto where the real magic begins.

The Elves Arrive

Your doorbell rings. The Elves are there to collect the children and walk them to the magical vehicle.

The Grotto Opens

The Rudolph-themed vehicle awaits. The automatic door opens to reveal Santa's Grotto inside.

Meet Santa

The children meet Father Christmas himself — a real, full VIP meet and greet experience.

Post Your Letter

Children post their letter direct into Santa's post box on the vehicle.

Story Time

Santa reads a Christmas story with the children inside the grotto.

Sing Along

Christmas songs with Santa and the Elves — a proper festive sing-along.

The Wish List

Children tell Santa their Christmas wish list directly — no middleman required.

Photo Opportunities

Plenty of time for photos with Santa and the Elves — memories to keep forever.

Naughty or Nice?

Every child receives their personalised Naughty or Nice Certificate from Santa.

The Gifts

Reindeer Dust, Hot Chocolate, Candy Cane and Santa's Gold Chocolate Coin for every child.

The Grinch!

The Grinch chases the children back inside — trying to steal their treats! (Optional — sorry, kids may cry! 😂)

Pricing

Simple, transparent pricing.

Dates and times organised at time of booking. Maximum 5 children per booking.

First Child

£49

The full experience — Elves, Santa, the Grotto, all gifts, the Grinch chase (optional). Includes the first child in the booking.

Each Additional Child

£9 per child

Added on top of the £49 first child price. Maximum 5 children per booking. Book early — dates fill quickly in December.

⚠️All workers are fully PVG & Disclosure checked. Parents are present for 100% of the experience — always.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Kennedy Executive Travel ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you make an enquiry or booking through this website, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Journey details — pick-up and drop-off locations, dates, times and passenger numbers
  • Any additional information you choose to provide, such as special requests

We do not collect payment information through this website. Any payments are arranged directly between you and us.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and confirm bookings
  • Plan and deliver the journey you've requested
  • Contact you about your booking, including changes or updates

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How we store your information

Enquiries submitted through this website are sent directly to our email address via a secure third-party form service (FormSubmit.co). We retain enquiry and booking information only for as long as necessary to fulfil your booking and for our own record-keeping.

Cookies & analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies. We may use privacy-friendly analytics (such as Umami) to understand general visitor numbers — this does not collect any personally identifiable information.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make a request, please contact us at kennedyexecutivetravel@gmail.com.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your information, please get in touch:

Email: kennedyexecutivetravel@gmail.com
Phone: 07961 834 904

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
